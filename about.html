Why CromMind

Technology matters most when people can use it.

Some people come to technology with a business problem they want to solve. Others come with a skill they want to learn. CromMind makes room for both.

Through the digital studio, we create websites, web applications, and software for businesses. Through the ICT Academy, learners can build skills in Digital Literacy, Web Design, Frontend Development, Backend Development, and Full Stack Development with AI Automation.

The work is different, but the idea behind it is related: make digital technology more useful, understandable, and within reach.

The person behind CromMind

Full-stack developer. Educator. Founder.

Stephen Ajao is the developer behind CromMind. He builds full-stack web applications with React, Node.js, Express, and MongoDB, including e-commerce platforms, authentication systems, and real-time voting and ranking tools. His work pairs responsive interfaces with structured backends and API security.

He also teaches web development at The Polytechnic, Ibadan, where he designs curricula and mentors students from fundamentals through production-ready code. Based in Ibadan, Nigeria, Stephen holds a Diploma in Full-Stack Development and an HND in Statistics.

A practical point of view

Keep the work close to real needs.

These ideas guide how the studio describes its work and how the Academy frames learning.

  • Start with people Understand who a product or learning experience is for before deciding what it should do.
  • Make it useful Choose technology to serve a clear purpose, not just because it is new.
  • Keep learning practical Build understanding through real skills and considered digital work.
